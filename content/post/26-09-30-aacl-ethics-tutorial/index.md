WING shared details of *The Future of NLP Needs Ethical Considerations: An Interactive Tutorial for Researchers and Students*, an ACL Ethics Committee tutorial scheduled for the first tutorial morning at AACL-IJCNLP 2026 on 9 November.

<!--more-->

The tutorial invited researchers and students to work through ethical questions that arise in natural language processing, including bias in language and representations, privacy, consent, fairness, research design, and the environmental impact of AI. Through interactive discussions and case studies, participants would consider when a paper may need ethics review and how to write a meaningful ethics-considerations section. Remote participants were also welcome.

The tutorial was presented in the AACL-IJCNLP 2026 tutorial proceedings. Its authors include WING researchers Min-Yen Kan, Yisong Miao, and Yajing Yang, alongside colleagues from the ACL Ethics Committee and the broader NLP community. Read the [tutorial abstract](https://aclanthology.org/2026.aacl-tutorials.2/) and access the [tutorial materials](https://ethics.aclweb.org/tutorials/AACL-IJCNLP_2026/).
