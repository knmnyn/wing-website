abstract: >-
  SpecEvo is a multi-LLM evolutionary framework for scientific discovery that explores with lightweight models and consolidates with frontier reasoning. Parallel Speculators propose programs, a trajectory-aware Advisor turns successes and failures into reusable guidance, and a frontier Navigator intervenes at sparse checkpoints, so that model coordination adapts as the search progresses rather than following fixed roles.
  Across 176 tasks in four domains, SpecEvo is competitive with frontier-model baselines on mathematical discovery and systems optimization at up to 80% lower API cost, achieves a 3.8% higher mean normalized score than the strongest baseline on CO-Bench combinatorial optimization, and attains the lowest NMSE in all eight LSR-Synth equation discovery settings among the main evolutionary baselines.
