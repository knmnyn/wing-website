abstract: 'Camellia is a benchmark for evaluating entity-centric cultural biases in large language models across nine Asian languages and six cultures. It includes 19,530 manually annotated entities and 2,173 masked social-media contexts. Evaluations across cultural context adaptation, sentiment association, and entity extractive question answering show challenges in cultural adaptation, model-specific sentiment associations, and context understanding gaps across Asian languages.'
